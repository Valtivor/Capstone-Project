Indices & Logarithms

WAEC heavily tests the fundamental laws governing powers and their relationships to logarithms.

Multiplication Law: \(a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}\)
Division Law: \(a^m \div a^n = a^{m-n}\)

Example: Simplify \(10^7 \div 10^3 = 10^{7-3} = 10^4\).

Quadratic Equations

An equation in the form \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\).

The General Formula: $$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

Remember: The discriminant is \(b^2 - 4ac\). If it evaluates to less than zero, the graph does not touch the x-axis.

Circle Geometry

WAEC theory questions frequently check core Circle Theorems.

Theorem 1: The angle subtended by an arc at the centre is twice the angle subtended at any point on the remaining part of the circumference.

Keep in mind that angles in the same segment of a circle are equal.

Trigonometry & Bearings

Deals with right-angled and non-right-angled triangle calculations.

Sine Rule: \(\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}\)
Cosine Rule: \(a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc \cos A\)
